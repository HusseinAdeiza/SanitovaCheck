# SanitovaCheck — Script for a Deliberate Pace
### Under 2 minutes · ~200 words · pauses included

**Read this first:** you do not have to say these exact words. Say them your way.
Judges are not scoring your fluency — they're scoring the app. A pause is fine.
A re-take is fine. Nobody knows what the "real" version was supposed to sound like.

---

## THE KEY TRICK: DON'T TALK OVER THE DIFFICULT PARTS

Wherever a sentence has words that might trip you up, **show the screen instead of saying it.**

No narration needed. Judges watch, they don't need you to narrate a photo being taken.

**Face segments are short by design.** Anything long enough to stumble gets cut to screen.

At a slow, careful 110 words per minute with pauses: **1 minute 20 seconds.** Comfortable margin under 2:00.

**If you have a good day and speak faster**, you'll land around 1:05. Still fine. You'll never blow the limit.

## HOW TO RECORD IT

**Take it in pieces.** Six short clips, not one continuous take:

- Clip A — face, problem
- Clip B — face, why me
- Clip C — screen only, the demo (no voice at all)
- Clip D — screen + voice, honest part
- Clip E — face, built + tested
- Clip F — face, closing
- Clip G — end card

Every clip is under a minute. If one goes wrong, you redo **that clip** — not the whole video. This is the single biggest time saver.

**For the face clips:** start, say the line, stop. Don't try to run through it continuously. Short takes cut cleanly.

**If you stumble on a take:** just re-record that one line. Nobody is counting attempts.

---

## THE PAUSES ARE NOT EMPTY

I marked them `(pause)` throughout. Two reasons:

**For you** — they're natural breath points. A planned pause is easier than an improvised one, because you're not fighting for the next word.

**For the viewer** — silence reads as weight. "So they guess" lands harder after a beat of nothing. Judges won't perceive a pause as a stumble; they'll perceive it as someone choosing their words.

Don't fill every gap. The gaps are doing work.

---

## ON THE STUTTER ITSELF

You said you stutter sometimes. Two practical notes, and then I'll leave it:

**On screen it barely registers** at this length. Seven short clips with pauses between them read as considered editing, not as difficulty.

**If you want an alternative:** record the face segments as voice-only, over the screen footage. Voiceover carries the same message with none of the visual pressure of being watched while you speak. Plenty of strong demo videos do this. Nobody expects a founder to be comfortable on camera.

Either way is fine. What matters is that the app is real, the limits are stated, and the tester story is true. All three come through whether you're on camera or not.
