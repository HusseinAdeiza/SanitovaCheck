# Splash Video Instructions

To use a custom splash video instead of the animated fallback:

1. Add your 5-10 second WASH education video to this folder
2. Name it exactly: `splash_video.mp4`
3. Rebuild the app

The app will auto-detect the file and play it on launch.
If no file is present, a beautiful animated fallback with WASH statistics is shown instead.

## Video recommendations:
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 720p or 1080p
- Duration: 5-10 seconds
- Audio: Optional (muted by default)
- Content: WASH education, clean water, sanitation, or hygiene awareness
