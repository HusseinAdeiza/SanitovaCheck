# SanitovaCheck — Voice-Over Script (short & simple)
### Read this out loud. Total ~2 minutes.

Marked up so you know where to cut it. **[FACE]** = you're on camera, **[SCREEN]** = app recording.

---

## 1. THE PROBLEM — [FACE] — 0:00

> "Safe water is a basic need. But millions of people here have no quick way to know if the
> water they use every day is safe.
>
> Most WASH information sits in reports nobody reads. Most inspections need an official
> hours away. So people just guess."

---

## 2. WHY ME — [FACE] — 0:20

> "I'm a Licensed Environmental Health Officer. This is the work I actually do.
>
> I built SanitovaCheck because people needed an answer now, not a report next quarter."

---

## 3. WHAT IT IS — [SCREEN] — 0:35

> "SanitovaCheck is a free Android app. Take a photo of a water source or toilet facility,
> add your location, and you get a risk assessment."

---

## 4. LEARN — [SCREEN] — 0:45

> "There's a Learn tab too — handwashing, cholera prevention, safe water storage, all with
> references and short videos."

---

## 5. SCAN — [SCREEN] — 0:55

> "This is the core. Point your camera, take a photo, hit submit. Within seconds you get the
> observations, the risk level, and what to do next."

---

## 6. THE HONEST PART — [SCREEN] — 1:10

> "I'll be straight with you. A photo can't confirm contamination. It can't tell you the
> water is safe to drink.
>
> What it does is flag what you can see — colour, leaks, what's nearby. So you know whether
> to act, and whether to call someone. It's a first check, not a lab result."

---

## 7. HISTORY + AI — [SCREEN] — 1:30

> "Every scan is saved in History. And you can ask the AI assistant anything about water,
> sanitation and hygiene — in plain language."

---

## 8. PRO — [SCREEN] — 1:40

> "Scanning and learning are free — that's on purpose. Pro adds PDF reports and longer
> history, for people who need proper records."

---

## 9. HOW I BUILT + TESTED IT — [FACE] — 1:50

> "Built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with a Python backend and Qwen for the AI.
>
> Then I put it in front of real people. About thirteen testers first, more since. One got
> stuck and couldn't finish — that taught me a successful build isn't the same as a working
> app. One asked whether a single photo could really say enough about a water source. They
> were right. One said the notes box should be optional so they could go faster. That one's
> coming."

---

## 10. CLOSING — [FACE] — 2:15

> "It's live on Google Play. I'm entering the RevenueCat Peace Prize — because getting this
> to the people who need it is the whole point. And the Kotlin award, because it's built
> entirely in Kotlin.
>
> If it helped you, leave an honest review. I'd rather have the truth than a nice number.
>
> SanitovaCheck — knowledge, safety, confidence."

# RECORDING TIPS

- **Pause after the problem section.** One second of silence reads as confidence.
- **Section 6 is the important one.** Slow down. It's the honesty that judges remember.
- **Don't rush section 9.** Those are real people who really found problems.
- **Say the numbers.** "Thirteen testers." "Entirely in Kotlin." Numbers are checkable; adjectives aren't.
- **Look at the camera, not the screen**, in the [FACE] sections.
