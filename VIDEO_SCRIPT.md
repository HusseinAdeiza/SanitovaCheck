# SanitovaCheck — Demo Video Script
### RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 · RevenueCat Peace Prize + Ship Kotlin Everywhere (JetBrains)

**Target length:** 2 min 30 s – 3 min
**Format:** You on camera + screen recording, cut together
**Upload to YouTube as:** Unlisted (never private)

---

## CAPTURE SETUP (do this before you record anything)

You need two sources. Pick one:

**Option A — Two devices (easiest)**
- Phone A: your Android phone, screen recording ON, propped up to show the app
- Phone B or laptop webcam: your face and voice
- Record separately, edit together in CapCut (free) or DaVinci Resolve

**Option B — One phone**
- Screen record the app walkthrough first
- Then open the front camera and record yourself talking
- Same app, two clips, cut together

**Screen recording on Android:** Quick Settings → Screen record. Set 1080p.

**Your setup:** neutral wall behind you, light in front of you (a window or a lamp — not
behind, that's the classic mistake), phone at eye level not chin level.

**VOICE-OVER (to camera, direct):**

> Most WASH information is buried in reports nobody reads. Most risk assessments belong

## SECTION 4 — THE APP (0:50 – 1:50)

### 4a. Learn tab (0:50 – 1:02)
**VISUAL:** Tap **Learn**. Scroll articles. Open one. Scroll to a video card.

**VOICE-OVER:**
> "The Learn tab has practical WASH articles — handwashing, cholera prevention, safe water
> storage — each with references, plus educational videos that open in YouTube."

### 4b. Scan — the core feature (1:02 – 1:32)
**VISUAL:** Tap **Scan** → **Start New Assessment** → camera on a water tap → capture →
observation field → **Use Current Location** → **Submit Assessment** → report appears.

**VOICE-OVER:**
> "This is the core. Point your camera at any water source or sanitation facility, take a
> photo, add a note and your location, and submit.
>
> Within seconds you get an AI-generated risk assessment — observations from the image, a
> risk indication, and suggested next steps."

### 4c. The honest part (1:32 – 1:45)
**VISUAL:** Stay on the report. Highlight the observations section.

**VOICE-OVER:**
> "I want to be straight about the limits, because this matters. A photograph cannot
> confirm microbial contamination and it cannot tell you the water is safe to drink.
>
> What it does is flag what you can see — discolouration, leaks, proximity to a
> contamination source — so you know whether to act and whether to escalate to a
> professional. It's a first check, not a laboratory result."

### 4d. History and export (1:45 – 1:50)
**VISUAL:** Home → History. Open a past report.

**VOICE-OVER:**
> "Every assessment is saved in Scan History, and Pro users can export a PDF report."

## SECTION 7 — HOW I BUILT IT + TESTED (2:10 – 2:40)
*Cut back to your face for this section. Real numbers, real feedback.*

**VOICE-OVER:**
> "I built this in Android Studio with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, CameraX for the camera,
> a Python backend on Alibaba Cloud Function Compute, and Qwen for the AI.
>
> Then I put it in front of real people through Google Play — about thirteen testers
> initially, and more since. Their feedback changed the app.
>

> useful part — I'd rather have honest feedback now than a nice number later."

## THINGS NOT TO SAY

- ❌ "AI verifies your water is safe" — it does not
- ❌ "Affiliated with WHO / UNICEF / CDC" — it is not
- ❌ "Certified" / "approved" / "validated" — it is not
- ❌ "Replace professional testing" — it does not
- ❌ "Unlimited scans" — not true
- ❌ Any claim about a feature you haven't personally verified on device

Keep the framing as "references to" their materials, never "in partnership with." Your
Play listing was already rejected once for misleading claims — a video makes that risk
worse, not better.
